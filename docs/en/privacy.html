Privacy policy

YOUR DATA STAYS YOURS.

Dealz never sells data. This page explains, plainly, what we collect, why, who we share it with and how to exercise your rights.

Last updated: 28 October 2026

1. Who is responsible?

Dealz, a sole proprietorship based in Switzerland, is the controller of your data under the Swiss Federal Act on Data Protection (FADP).

Contact for any question or request: info@dealz.website

2. Our commitment

  • We never sell or rent personal data, to anyone.
  • We use no advertising cookies and no profiling. We only measure the site's audience with Google Analytics (see section 4).
  • We collect only what is needed for the service you ask for.

4. Who do we share your data with?

Only with the technical providers without which the service would not work. They process data on our behalf under their own security and confidentiality commitments:

  • Vercel: hosting of the site and the service.
  • Supabase: database holding quotes, requests and contacts.
  • Anthropic: artificial-intelligence processing of the content of your conversations with the assistant, to generate replies.
  • Meta (WhatsApp): delivery of WhatsApp messages.
  • Stripe: card payments.
  • An email delivery service (SMTP): quotes, confirmations and replies.
  • Google Fonts: the site's typeface (your IP address is sent to Google when the page loads).
  • Google Analytics: audience measurement for the site (pages viewed, device, origin). Google sets cookies and receives your IP address. You can refuse them in your browser settings or use Google's opt-out add-on.

We share your data with no other third party, except where the law requires it.

5. Data abroad

Some of these providers are located, or store data, outside Switzerland (notably in the European Union and the United States). Where that is the case, we rely on appropriate safeguards, such as standard contractual clauses or an adequacy decision, as required by the FADP.

6. How long?

We keep your data as long as needed for the service you asked for, then delete or anonymise it. Accounting records are kept for the legal period of ten years. On request we delete your data, unless the law requires us to keep it.

7. Outreach and objection

If we contact you in a business capacity, you can object at any time: reply STOP to a WhatsApp message or write to info@dealz.website. We stop all contact immediately and keep your details on a suppression list so we do not approach you again.

8. Your rights

You may request access to your data, its correction, deletion, restriction or objection to its processing, and a copy in a common format. Write to info@dealz.website; we reply within 30 days.

You can also lodge a complaint with the Federal Data Protection and Information Commissioner (FDPIC), edoeb.admin.ch.

9. Security

We protect your data with appropriate technical and organisational measures: encrypted connections (HTTPS), restricted access, established providers. No system is infallible; if a breach poses a risk to you, we will notify you and the competent authority as the law requires.

10. Changes

We may update this policy, for example if we add a tool. The version on this page, with its update date, is always the one in force.
